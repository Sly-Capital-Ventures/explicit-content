# Article Format

The house style for a research article on explicitsarms.com: a **claim-free spec sheet**, not a blog post that sells a benefit. Mirror the existing Explicit Research articles — neutral, factual, reference-grade. Read `compliance.md` first; nothing here overrides it.

## Frontmatter (required, matches the CMS fields)
```
---
title: "<Compound name — the H1. No claims.>"
slug: "<lowercase-hyphenated, matches the compound>"
meta_title: "<~60 chars>"
meta_description: "<~155 chars, identity/catalog facts only>"
focus_keyword: "<the primary term, optional>"
date: "<YYYY-MM-DD>"
image: "<optional featured image path>"
---
```

## Section structure (in order)

1. **Intro (2–4 sentences).** What *class* of molecule it is and where its sequence occurs in nature or research literature. Neutral identity only. e.g. *"BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a sequence identified in gastric juice, and one of the most widely published subjects in peptide research."* — never "studied for [benefit]".

2. **Specifications** (a table — this is the value of a spec sheet):
   | Field | Example |
   |---|---|
   | Type / class | Synthetic peptide |
   | Sequence | (if known) |
   | Molecular formula | |
   | Molecular weight | |
   | CAS number | |
   | PubChem CID | (link to the PubChem page) |
   | Appearance | Lyophilized powder |

3. **Research context (neutral).** That it appears as a *subject* in research literature and what field studies it (e.g. "peptide research", "cell-biology research"). Describe the research *area*, never an outcome or benefit. No "studied for recovery/healing/etc."

4. **Quality & testing.** Independently third-party tested; COA available. Purity % only where true (≥99% on actives; "research grade" on blends; never on bacteriostatic water). No blanket country-of-origin.

5. **Available formats.** Link the real product + sizes from `product-catalog.md` (`https://explicitsarms.com/products/<slug>.html`). State sizes/price as catalog facts.

6. **Related** (internal links). Cross-reference related products and the matching **Explicit Research** article for the same compound (the cross-reference strategy — link, don't duplicate).

7. **Research-use disclaimer** (always): "For laboratory and research use only. Not for human or animal consumption."

## Scope
Write only for **IN-SCOPE peptides** in `product-catalog.md`. SARMs, SERMs, AIs, and GLP compounds are excluded — do not write these unless explicitly approved.

## Length & depth
Depth comes from **specs and accurate identity**, not word count. A tight, correct spec sheet beats a padded article. No filler, no fabricated facts to hit a length.
